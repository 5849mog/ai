02 / A MOVE FOR YOU

Move
suggestions.

Ask for suggestions to compare possible moves for the side to play. Pick the move you want to make.

SuggestionsUp to 1 s

Compare the top move with an alternative when available.

AI turnsFixed or adaptive

0.1, 0.5, 1, 5, or 10 seconds, or an adaptive budget up to 6 seconds.

Both use Rapfi with mix9svq. The search may finish early when it finds a winning move.

  1. 01

    Read the board

    Send the current position and side to move to the engine.

  2. 02

    Compare lines

    Evaluate patterns and compare replies for both sides within the time budget.

  3. 03

    Choose a move

    The first suggestion is the engine’s preferred move in this search. The second is an alternative.

How often would the top suggestion win?

This exact matchup has no published win rate.

Following every top suggestion pits the same engine against itself under different search settings. Suggestions use up to one second. The opponent’s budget depends on the setting you choose.

A useful estimate would come from complete games with fixed settings and repeated openings with colors swapped. Hardware and background search can affect the result.

Why is there sometimes only one suggestion?

Some opening stages and immediate wins produce one suggestion. The search can also finish before it has a complete pair of candidates.

What does the win probability show?

It shows the engine’s estimate for the current board. It updates as the position or evaluation changes.

What happens to suggestions after undo?

A move, undo, new game, swap, or import clears the previous suggestions. Use the suggestion button again to hide them.

02 / ENGINEGPL-3.0-or-later

Rapfi

The open-source Gomoku and Renju engine behind the board.

The browser build pins Rapfi 250615. The published patches fix thread-member access in the single-threaded build and exclude the unused mix10 evaluator from non-SIMD builds. They preserve mix9svq search and evaluation.

03 / NEURAL NETWORKCC0-1.0

mix9svq

The neural network evaluates board patterns. The weights are released under CC0-1.0.

Freestyle and Renju use separate pinned weights. The app verifies the SHA-256 of a reassembled model. Coordinates agree across the board, rules, and search.
